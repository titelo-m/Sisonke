How it works

Three simple steps to start borrowing or lending tools in your neighbourhood.

1. List your tools

Add the tools you already own. Set a small deposit fee and your suburb so neighbours can find them.

2. Find a tool nearby

Browse tools listed in your area. Filter by category, suburb, and price to find exactly what you need.

3. Request and borrow

Pick your dates and send a request. Once the owner approves, you arrange pickup and return the tool when done.
